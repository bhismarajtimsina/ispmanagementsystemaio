# Implementation Roadmap

Phase 1: tenancy, identity, subscription control, customers, billing foundation and canonical fiber topology.
Phase 2: RADIUS/PPPoE/HotSpot and MikroTik integration.
Phase 3: distributed NMS collectors, monitoring, topology and alarms.
Phase 4: OLT/ONU model framework, Huawei adapter first, registration and optical state.
Phase 5: full Fiber GIS/ODN operational workflows, OTDR and field PWA.
Phase 6: TR-069/ACS, followed by USP.
Phase 7: reseller commerce and customer portal.
Phase 8: configuration automation, approvals and rollback.
Phase 9: multi-region scale, HA and advanced analytics.
